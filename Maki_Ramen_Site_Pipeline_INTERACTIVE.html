Model notes & health warnings

Read these before quoting any single number. Prepared August 2026, internal.

This is a live capacity-led model. Revenue is built bottom-up: covers = seats × seat-turns × 7 × utilisation, where utilisation maps a weighted demand index (footfall, catchment, students, affluence, whitespace, evening economy, delivery) onto a 28–95% band. Spend per head = base + slope × affluence. It re-ranks the moment you change a lever.
